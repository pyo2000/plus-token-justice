What we are asking for

Three clear answers for PlusToken victims

01

Account for seized assets

Publish an official, verifiable account of assets seized in the PlusToken case and their current status.

02

Explain the legal process

Clarify which authorities are responsible and what decisions have been made about those assets.

03

Provide a path to restitution

Tell victims whether they can submit claims, what evidence is needed, and how to check progress.

This campaign requests transparency and a fair process. It does not claim that a specific distribution or recovery is guaranteed.
