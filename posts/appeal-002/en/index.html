Chinese authorities: publish a clear path to restitution

English · 한국어 · Deutsch · 中文

2026-10-21

People affected by PlusToken need a reliable source of information about any restitution process. We ask Chinese authorities and the agencies responsible for the case to publish official guidance, eligibility criteria, required documents, and a way to check claims.

A clear public process can reduce confusion and protect victims from false recovery offers. Please rely on official notices and never pay an upfront fee to someone promising guaranteed recovery.
