Chinese authorities: victims still need clear answers

English · 한국어 · Deutsch · 中文

Years have passed, yet many people affected by PlusToken still have unanswered questions about the case and assets reportedly seized in connection with it.

We respectfully call on Chinese authorities and the agencies responsible for the case to provide a transparent, verifiable public account of those assets and explain whether a restitution process exists, who is eligible, and how claims may be submitted.

Victims deserve accurate information, fair treatment, and access to a clear process. Please share verified updates and keep this appeal peaceful and respectful.
